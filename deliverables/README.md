## The story in one paragraph

AtliQ does not have a lead problem; it has a memory problem. Only 12 of 37 client commitments due by 10 July 2026 were met, and $423k of open pipeline is waiting on a reply. PromiseKeeper reads the team's emails, meeting notes and CRM, finds every promise, client deadline and buying signal with a verbatim quote from its source, and gives each seller a ranked daily brief. It proposes CRM fixes for one-click approval and never contacts a client or changes the CRM on its own. On a hand-labelled answer key it finds 37 of 37 open commitments with 90% precision and passes 5 of 5 safety tests, for about $60 a month in production.
